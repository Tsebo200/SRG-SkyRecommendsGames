# How to Test Your Own App via TestFlight 📱

## Quick Answer

**You test your beta app through the TestFlight app on your iPhone/iPad, NOT through the App Store.**

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Install TestFlight App

On your iPhone or iPad:

1. **Open App Store**
2. **Search**: "TestFlight"
3. **Install**: TestFlight app (by Apple)
   - It's free
   - Official Apple app for beta testing
   - Direct link: https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664

---

### Step 2: Add Yourself as a Tester

You need to add your own email address as a tester in App Store Connect:

1. **Go to App Store Connect**: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. **Login** with: `tbonziser@gmail.com`
3. **Click**: "My Apps"
4. **Select**: Your app (SRG)
5. **Click**: "TestFlight" tab (at the top)
6. **Wait for**: Build to show "Ready to Test" status
   - Current status: Processing (check the link above)
   - You'll receive an email when ready
7. **Click**: "Internal Testing" (left sidebar)
8. **Click**: "+" button or "Add Testers"
9. **Enter**: Your email address: `tbonziser@gmail.com`
10. **Click**: "Add" or "Invite"

**Note**: Sometimes app owners are automatically added to internal testing. Check Step 3 first!

---

### Step 3: Accept Invitation & Install

#### Option A: Via Email

1. **Check your email**: `tbonziser@gmail.com`
2. **Look for**: Email from Apple/TestFlight
   - Subject: "You've been invited to test [App Name]"
3. **Open email on your iPhone/iPad**
4. **Tap**: "View in TestFlight" button
5. **TestFlight app opens** (or installs if needed)
6. **Tap**: "Install" button next to your app
7. **App installs on your device!** ✅

#### Option B: Via TestFlight App

1. **Open TestFlight app** on your iPhone/iPad
2. **Check**: If your app appears in the list
   - If yes: Tap "Install" ✅
   - If no: Go back to Step 2 to add yourself
3. **Tap**: "Install" button
4. **App installs on your device!** ✅

---

## 📍 Where to Find Your Beta App

### ✅ In TestFlight App:
- Open TestFlight app
- Your app appears in the list
- Tap to install/update

### ❌ NOT in App Store:
- Won't appear in App Store search
- Not in "Your Apps" in App Store
- Only visible in TestFlight app

## 💡 Tips

1. **Test on Real Device**: TestFlight only works on physical devices (iPhone/iPad), not simulators
2. **Same Apple ID**: Use the same Apple ID that you use for App Store Connect
3. **Check Email**: Invitation might go to spam folder
4. **Auto-Updates**: TestFlight automatically notifies you of new builds
5. **Feedback**: You can send feedback directly from TestFlight app

---

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### App Doesn't Appear in TestFlight?
- **Check**: Build status is "Ready to Test"
- **Verify**: You added yourself as tester
- **Wait**: Sometimes takes a few minutes after adding
- **Refresh**: Pull down to refresh TestFlight app

### No Email Invitation?
- **Check spam folder**
- **Verify email**: Make sure you added the correct email
- **Check App Store Connect**: See if you're already in the tester list
- **Try**: Opening TestFlight app directly - you might already be added

### Can't Install?
- **Check**: You're on a physical device (not simulator)
- **Verify**: iOS version is compatible
- **Try**: Deleting and reinstalling TestFlight app
- **Check**: Device storage space
