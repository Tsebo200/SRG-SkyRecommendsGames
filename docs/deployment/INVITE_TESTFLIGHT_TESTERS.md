# How to Invite Testers to TestFlight 🧪

## 📱 Important: TestFlight vs App Store

### ❌ Beta App is NOT in the App Store
- Your beta version does **NOT** appear in the App Store
- Testers **cannot** find it by searching the App Store
- It's completely separate from the public App Store

### ✅ Beta App is in TestFlight App
- Testers need to install the **TestFlight app** (from App Store)
- You send them email invitations
- They accept and install your beta from TestFlight app
- TestFlight is Apple's beta testing platform

---

## 🎯 Step-by-Step: Invite Testers

### Step 1: Wait for Build to Process ✅

Your build is currently being processed by Apple:
- **Status**: Processing (5-30 minutes)
- **Check**: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/apps/6757149381/testflight/ios
- **Wait for**: Status to change to "Ready to Test"

You'll receive an email when it's ready!

---

### Step 2: Go to App Store Connect

1. **Open**: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. **Login** with your Apple ID: `tbonziser@gmail.com`
3. **Click**: "My Apps"
4. **Select**: Your app (SRG)
5. **Click**: "TestFlight" tab (at the top)

---

### Step 3: Choose Testing Type

You'll see two options:

#### Option A: Internal Testing (Recommended First)
- ✅ **Up to 100 testers**
- ✅ **No App Review required**
- ✅ **Immediate access** (as soon as build is ready)
- ✅ **Best for**: Team members, friends, close testers

#### Option B: External Testing
- ✅ **Up to 10,000 testers**
- ⚠️ **Requires App Review** (24-48 hours for first build)
- ✅ **Best for**: Public beta, larger audience
- ⚠️ **Requires**: App information, screenshots, privacy policy

**Recommendation**: Start with **Internal Testing** first!

---

### Step 4: Add Internal Testers

1. **Click**: "Internal Testing" (left sidebar)
2. **Click**: "+" button (top right) or "Add Testers"
3. **Enter email addresses**:
   - Type email addresses (one per line or comma-separated)
   - Or import from contacts
   - Example: `tester1@example.com`, `tester2@example.com`
4. **Click**: "Add" or "Invite"
5. **Done!** Testers will receive email invitations

---

### Step 5: Testers Receive Email

Testers will get an email that looks like:
```
Subject: You've been invited to test [App Name]

You've been invited to test [App Name] on TestFlight.

[View in TestFlight] button
```

---

### Step 6: Testers Install Your Beta

Testers need to:

1. **Install TestFlight app** (if they don't have it):
   - Go to App Store
   - Search "TestFlight"
   - Install the TestFlight app (by Apple)

2. **Accept invitation**:
   - Open the email on their iPhone/iPad
   - Tap "View in TestFlight" button
   - Or open TestFlight app and accept invitation

3. **Install your beta app**:
   - Your app appears in TestFlight app
   - Tap "Install" button
   - App installs on their device
   - They can now test your app!

## 💡 Tips

- **Start small**: Add 2-3 testers first to test the process
- **Internal testing**: No review needed, instant access
- **External testing**: Requires review, but can reach more people
- **Feedback**: Testers can send feedback directly from TestFlight
- **Updates**: When you submit new builds, testers get notified automatically

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Testers Don't Receive Email?
- Check spam folder
- Verify email address is correct
- Make sure they have an Apple ID with that email

### Build Not Showing "Ready to Test"?
- Wait a bit longer (can take up to 30 minutes)
- Check email for processing completion
- Refresh App Store Connect page

### Can't Add Testers?
- Make sure build status is "Ready to Test"
- Verify you're in the correct testing group
- Check you have admin access to the app
